Why We Built TapIn

Every semester, students arriving in university towns like Nairobi, Juja, Eldoret, and Nyeri face the same recurring challenge: unregulated housing brokers demanding exorbitant viewing fees, unverified listings, and insecure rental deposits.

We created TapIn to turn this fragmented search into a transparent, digital-first experience. By pairing geolocation technology, digital tenancy agreements, and direct landlord communication, we ensure every student finds a secure home to thrive in their studies.

Platform Verification Framework

How we ensure transparency, authenticity, and peace of mind for every listing

01

Host Identity & Ownership Check

Landlords undergo national ID verification and submit proof of property management or title rights before any room listing is approved for publication.

02

On-Site Premises Inspection

Units are inspected for realistic photography, working water and electricity utilities, perimeter gate security, and walking distance to university facilities.

03

Protected Digital Agreements

Standardized digital tenancy agreements are generated with verified terms, ensuring both student tenants and hosts have clear, enforceable legal documentation.
